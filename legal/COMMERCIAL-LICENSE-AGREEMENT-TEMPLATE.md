# AIFENCE Commercial License Agreement — counsel-review template

> This template is not an offer and grants no rights. It must be completed and approved by qualified counsel for both parties. A commercial license becomes effective only when signed by authorized representatives and accompanied by an executed order form.

## 1. Parties and order form

This agreement is between the AIFENCE copyright holder identified in the applicable order form (“Licensor”) and the customer identified in that order form (“Customer”). The order form defines the effective date, term, fees, licensed entities, deployment scope, usage limits, support plan, and any additional terms.

## 2. Commercial license grant

Subject to payment and compliance, Licensor grants Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable license during the term to install, execute, reproduce, and internally modify the identified AIFENCE server software for the licensed scope.

Rights to embed, redistribute, sublicense, provide managed services, operate for affiliates or third parties, or create OEM products apply only when expressly included in the order form.

## 3. Restrictions

Except as expressly authorized, Customer may not:

- Transfer, sublicense, sell, rent, or distribute the software
- Use the software beyond licensed entities, systems, regions, capacity, or purpose
- Remove copyright, license, trademark, attribution, security, or provenance notices
- Circumvent license controls or usage limits
- Represent a modified build as an official AIFENCE release
- Use Licensor’s signing identities, certification marks, or release attestations for modified software

Restrictions do not limit rights that cannot legally be restricted.

## 4. Ownership

Licensor and its contributors retain all rights in AIFENCE. Customer retains rights in Customer-authored modifications, subject to Licensor’s ownership of the underlying software. No rights are granted by implication.

## 5. Open-source components

Third-party and separately identified open-source components remain governed by their own licenses. Apache-licensed AIFENCE SDKs and API materials remain available under Apache-2.0.

## 6. Delivery, updates, and support

Delivery format, update rights, support hours, severity definitions, response targets, and service levels are defined in the order form or support schedule. Unless expressly stated, the software is self-hosted and Licensor does not operate Customer environments.

## 7. Security and privacy

Each party is responsible for security obligations allocated in the order form and data-processing terms. Customer controls deployment configuration, identities, keys, policies, integrations, retention, and operational qualification unless managed services are expressly purchased.

## 8. Verification and records

Customer will maintain records reasonably sufficient to demonstrate compliance with licensed scope. Licensor may request a written compliance certification and, no more than annually unless a material discrepancy is found, conduct a confidential audit through an independent auditor under reasonable safeguards.

## 9. Confidentiality

Each party will protect the other party’s non-public confidential information using at least reasonable care and use it only to perform the agreement. Standard exclusions apply for information independently developed, publicly available without breach, rightfully received from another source, or required to be disclosed by law.

## 10. Warranties

Any limited performance, authority, malware, or professional-services warranties must be stated in the order form. Except for express warranties, the software is provided “AS IS” to the maximum extent permitted by law.

## 11. Indemnification

Any intellectual-property, security, data-protection, or customer-use indemnities, exclusions, procedures, and remedies must be negotiated in the order form. No indemnity is created by this template.

## 12. Limitation of liability

Liability caps, excluded damages, super-caps, and uncapped obligations must be stated in the signed agreement. No liability allocation is created by this template.

## 13. Term and termination

The agreement continues for the order-form term. A party may terminate for uncured material breach after the agreed cure period, insolvency where lawful, or other stated causes. On termination, Customer must stop unlicensed use and destroy or return commercial-only materials, subject to archival and legal-retention exceptions.

## 14. Export and sanctions

Each party will comply with applicable export-control and sanctions laws. Customer will not deploy or transfer the software where prohibited.

## 15. General terms

The signed agreement should address governing law, venue, notices, assignment, subcontracting, force majeure, order of precedence, waiver, severability, amendments, and counterparts.
