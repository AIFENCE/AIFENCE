# Brand Identity / Logo Production Contract
<!-- id: contract.artifact.brand-identity -->

## Identity Strategy
Resolve brand character, audience, category conventions, differentiation, use environments, naming constraints, and trademark/search caveats before finalizing visual directions.

## Direction Exploration
Explore materially different identity directions before convergence. A logo variation is not a new direction if concept, silhouette, typography, and visual logic are unchanged.

## System Contract
Define primary/secondary marks, wordmark behavior, lockups, clear space, minimum size, color variants, monochrome use, typography, palette, icon/graphic language, and misuse rules as applicable.

## Originality Boundary
Reference brands may contribute abstract principles only. Do not trace, imitate, or reproduce protected marks or distinctive trade dress.

## Evidence
Inspect small-size legibility, monochrome performance, light/dark use, horizontal/vertical contexts, favicon/avatar crops, contrast, and export/vector integrity where produced.
