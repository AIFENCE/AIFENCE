# Marketing Website Production Contract
<!-- id: contract.artifact.marketing-website -->

## User Jobs
<!-- id: contract.artifact.marketing-website.user-jobs -->
A visitor should understand the offering, determine fit, establish sufficient trust, answer material objections, and take the appropriate next action without template filler.

## Required Information Outcomes
<!-- id: contract.artifact.marketing-website.information -->
Resolve identity/value, audience fit, offerings, truthful proof or trust substitutes, engagement process, meaningful objections, conversion paths, contact expectations, and triggered legal/privacy/discoverability requirements. For complex-consideration B2B work, also resolve decision paths for evaluation/fit plus material adoption, implementation, integration, security, procurement, or handoff risk as applicable. Section order is not fixed.

## Feature Obligations
<!-- id: contract.artifact.marketing-website.features -->
Navigation, CTAs, forms, disclosures, search, galleries, pricing, FAQs, and other material interactive features must be compiled. Forms require validation, truthful submission behavior, error/success, loading/disabled, and front-end-only semantics where applicable.

## Component Grammar
<!-- id: contract.artifact.marketing-website.components -->
Do not express every information type with equal rounded cards. Choose editorial text, media, proof, structured lists, process, comparison, statistics, callouts, or other grammars according to content and domain.

## Responsive Contract
<!-- id: contract.artifact.marketing-website.responsive -->
Hero, media, navigation, proof, conversion, and dense content must be intentionally recomposed on narrow viewports.

## Truth Contract
<!-- id: contract.artifact.marketing-website.truth -->
Unknown testimonials, awards, locations, certifications, customer counts, prices, guarantees, project history, service areas, and results must not be invented.

## Evidence
<!-- id: contract.artifact.marketing-website.evidence -->
Production evidence should include desktop/mobile rendered review, primary conversion verification, meaningful states, broken-path checks, and applicable accessibility checks.

## Acceptance Profile
<!-- id: contract.artifact.marketing-website.acceptance -->
Use `quality-floors.profile.marketing-website`. Premium public work targets 95+ overall after all required floors pass.
