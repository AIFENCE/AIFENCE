# Regulated Public Interface Overlay Contract
<!-- id: contract.artifact.regulated-public-interface -->

This is additive for healthcare, financial, legal, insurance, government, safety-sensitive, or other materially regulated public experiences.

## Truth & Claim Boundary
<!-- id: contract.artifact.regulated-public-interface.claims -->
Do not invent credentials, licensure, outcomes, rates, eligibility, guarantees, diagnoses, financial performance, legal outcomes, compliance certifications, coverage, pricing, service availability, or professional qualifications.

## Risk & Privacy Contract
<!-- id: contract.artifact.regulated-public-interface.risk -->
Resolve privacy/security/legal routing before collecting sensitive information. Forms/CTAs must not imply secure intake, protected communications, advice, eligibility, or transactional capability that implementation does not provide.

## Accessibility Contract
<!-- id: contract.artifact.regulated-public-interface.accessibility -->
Accessibility is release-blocking for critical public information and action paths.

## Decision-Safety Contract
<!-- id: contract.artifact.regulated-public-interface.decision-safety -->
Distinguish education, marketing, professional services, urgent/safety guidance when applicable, and actual transactional/clinical/legal/financial actions.

## Evidence
<!-- id: contract.artifact.regulated-public-interface.evidence -->
Review claims, forms, data collection, consent/privacy implications, accessible completion, risk overlays, backend truth, and high-risk wording.

## Acceptance Profile
<!-- id: contract.artifact.regulated-public-interface.acceptance -->
Apply `quality-floors.profile.regulated-overlay`, which raises truthfulness and accessibility floors.
