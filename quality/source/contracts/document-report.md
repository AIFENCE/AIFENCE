# Document / Report Production Contract
<!-- id: contract.artifact.document-report -->

## Reader Jobs
<!-- id: contract.artifact.document-report.reader-jobs -->
A reader should understand the question, evidence, findings, implications, decisions, caveats, and next actions at appropriate depth.

## Information Contract
<!-- id: contract.artifact.document-report.information -->
Resolve purpose/audience, executive summary when useful, evidence versus interpretation, findings, analysis, risks/caveats, recommendations/decision, owners/timing when appropriate, references when required, and useful appendices.

## Visual Contract
<!-- id: contract.artifact.document-report.visual -->
Use hierarchy, tables, callouts, charts, diagrams, page structure, and whitespace according to information purpose. Do not decorate every finding as a card.

## Truth & Attribution
<!-- id: contract.artifact.document-report.truth -->
Distinguish supplied facts, calculations, external sources, assumptions, sample data, and recommendations. Do not manufacture citations or evidence.

## Output Integrity
<!-- id: contract.artifact.document-report.output -->
For fixed-format delivery, verify pagination, clipping, table overflow, heading continuity, links, image quality, and readability. For web documents, also validate responsive behavior.


## Decision-Depth Contract
<!-- id: contract.artifact.document-report.decision-depth -->
Use `DOCUMENT_CRAFT.md`. Score depth by decision question, evidence chain, analytical specificity, implications, risks/alternatives, recommendation, actions, and traceability—not UI interaction counts.

## Editorial Craft Contract
<!-- id: contract.artifact.document-report.editorial-craft -->
The information architecture and visual grammar must fit the document type. Use purposeful tables/diagrams/timelines/matrices and long-form hierarchy; do not reuse one generic report-card shell.

## Acceptance Profile
<!-- id: contract.artifact.document-report.acceptance -->
Use `quality-floors.profile.document-report`; mark truly non-applicable dimensions N/A rather than inventing evidence.
