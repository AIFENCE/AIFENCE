# Marketing Creative Production Contract
<!-- id: contract.artifact.marketing-creative -->

## Campaign Context
Resolve channel, placement, audience, objective, offer, mandatory copy/assets, format constraints, and success signal.

## Creative Direction
Generate differentiated concepts with a clear visual idea, message hierarchy, focal point, brand fit, and channel-native composition rather than generic promotional cards.

## Adaptation Contract
Plan how the concept adapts across required aspect ratios, crops, motion/static variants, copy lengths, safe areas, and platform overlays.

## Reference Boundary
Named references are translated into abstract qualities and constraints; protected compositions, characters, logos, and distinctive trade dress are not cloned.

## Evidence
Inspect hierarchy at real display size, safe areas, text legibility, crop resilience, brand consistency, factual claims, and export dimensions.
