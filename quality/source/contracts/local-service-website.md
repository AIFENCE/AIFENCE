# Local Service Website Production Contract
<!-- id: contract.artifact.local-service-website -->

## User Jobs
<!-- id: contract.artifact.local-service-website.user-jobs -->
A local prospect should quickly understand service fit, likely engagement process, credibility, and how to request service or a quote from a mobile device.

## Required Information Outcomes
<!-- id: contract.artifact.local-service-website.information -->
Resolve service categories/scope, audience/property/project fit, process, proof-bearing media when truthful, service/quote/contact path, supplied service-area/availability facts, objections, and trust without fabricated ratings, years, licenses, badges, or locations.

## Conversion & Contact
<!-- id: contract.artifact.local-service-website.conversion -->
Primary conversion should fit the business model: call, quote request, consultation, scheduling request, or contact. Mobile actions must be obvious and touch-appropriate. Do not invent a booking backend.

## Visual Contract
<!-- id: contract.artifact.local-service-website.visual -->
Use category-authentic media, craft/process detail, spatial composition, or service-specific evidence rather than SaaS-style universal icon cards.

## Feature & State Contract
<!-- id: contract.artifact.local-service-website.states -->
Quote/contact forms, galleries, service selectors, FAQs, maps, and disclosures require meaningful states and truthful behavior when used.

## Evidence
<!-- id: contract.artifact.local-service-website.evidence -->
Verify mobile conversion, navigation, forms/links, proof-media treatment, responsive crops, local-fact truthfulness, and desktop/mobile rendered quality.

## Acceptance Profile
<!-- id: contract.artifact.local-service-website.acceptance -->
Use `quality-floors.profile.local-service`. Genericity resistance and truthfulness are release-blocking.
