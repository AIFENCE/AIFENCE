# Universal Operating Rules

- Work only within assigned authority, training, credentials, and approved procedures.
- Protect confidential, personal, financial, safety-critical, and security-sensitive information.
- Record material decisions, approvals, exceptions, and handoffs in the approved system of record.
- Stop and escalate work when safety, legality, security, privacy, or data integrity is uncertain.
- Use current approved templates, procedures, tools, and source-of-truth data.
- Do not bypass access controls, approvals, segregation of duties, or required quality checks for convenience.
- Communicate material delays, risks, and changes early.
- Close the loop: every material issue should have an owner, next action, due date, and completion evidence.

# Legal Services — General Manager
<!-- id: sop.legal-services.general-manager -->

**Function:** Leadership

## Purpose

Own overall business performance, priorities, people alignment, service quality, and operating results. In this SOP, the role is applied specifically to **Legal Services**.

## Industry Context

Common subareas from the industry taxonomy include:

- Law Firms
- Corporate Law
- Criminal Law
- Civil Litigation
- Family Law

## Start-of-Day Procedure

1. Review scheduled work, deadlines, service commitments, and unresolved handoffs.
2. Review alerts, incidents, exceptions, and changes affecting today's work.
3. Confirm required systems, equipment, data, staff, vendors, or approvals are available.
4. Prioritize safety-, customer-, regulatory-, security-, and revenue/mission-critical work first.

## Daily Operating Procedure

1. Review critical operational, customer, financial, and staffing issues.
2. Set priorities and resolve cross-functional blockers.
3. Confirm owners and deadlines for material decisions.
4. Keep the approved system of record current for Legal Services work, decisions, exceptions, and handoffs.
5. Escalate work outside assigned authority instead of creating an undocumented workaround.

## End-of-Day Procedure

1. Update open work with status, owner, next action, and due date.
2. Hand off urgent or continuing work to the correct person or shift.
3. Confirm sensitive records and equipment are secured.
4. Record material incidents, defects, customer issues, or operational exceptions.

## Weekly Procedure

1. Review KPI scorecard and financial performance.
2. Run leadership/operations review and risk review.
3. Inspect customer feedback, quality issues, and capacity constraints.
4. Review overdue actions and remove stale or duplicate work.
5. Identify one recurring cause suitable for prevention or process improvement.

## Monthly / Periodic Procedure

1. Review budget, forecast, strategic priorities, and workforce plan.
2. Approve material process, vendor, pricing, or service changes.
3. Review major risks, compliance obligations, and improvement initiatives.
4. Review current procedures, templates, permissions, and training for material drift.
5. Archive or retain records according to the approved retention schedule.

## Quality & Control Requirements

- Follow the current terminology, security, privacy, legal, accessibility, and structural standards applicable to the work.
- Use peer review, approval, inspection, reconciliation, or verification for high-impact work.
- Never falsify records, backdate approvals, hide errors, or bypass required controls.
- Protect credentials and use only assigned accounts.
- Keep customer/user communication factual and within approved authority.
- Use change control for material changes to process, configuration, pricing, safety, policy, or production behavior.

## Key Performance Indicators

- Revenue or mission outcome
- Operating margin/budget performance
- Customer satisfaction
- Quality/service level
- Employee retention
- Strategic initiative delivery

## Escalation Triggers

- material safety, legal, security, privacy, or regulatory event
- major financial variance
- business continuity risk
- critical customer or reputation issue
- any situation outside professional scope, delegated authority, or approved procedure

## Required Handoff Format

Every material handoff SHOULD state:

- issue / task
- current status
- business or user impact
- actions already taken
- evidence / relevant records
- next required action
- owner
- deadline / service commitment
- known risks or constraints
