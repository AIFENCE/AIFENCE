# Operations 2.0 reference

Material SOPs, runbooks, work instructions, operational role specifications, decision-rights systems, or governed KPI systems activate Domain 31 alongside Domain 23 baseline coverage. Domain 31 specializes shared concepts; do not emit duplicate SOP/evidence/KPI/handoff objects.

Generated procedures begin as draft/general guidance unless organization approval or external authority is evidenced. Unknown approval limits, thresholds, credentials, system names, retention rules, jurisdictional requirements, and manufacturer steps remain explicit unknowns. Use `aifence_quality_compile_operation` and validate structured procedures with `aifence_quality_validate` when possible.
