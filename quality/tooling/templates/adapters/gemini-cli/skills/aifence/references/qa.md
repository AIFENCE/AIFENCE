# QA reference

For substantial work, validate the resolved artifact contract and applicable quality floors. Inspect the actual artifact, not only the plan. Interfaces require responsive, accessibility, states, dead-control, truth, implementation, and visual/genericity evidence as applicable. Documents require decision/evidence depth and traceability. Operations require schema/semantic validation, authority, decision rights, evidence/definition-of-done, exception/recovery, KPI governance, and lifecycle checks.

Fix avoidable P0/P1 or required-dimension failures before delivery. Do not average away a failed required dimension.
