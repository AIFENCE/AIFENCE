# Presentation / Deck Production Contract
<!-- id: contract.artifact.presentation-deck -->

## Audience & Decision Contract
Resolve the audience, meeting context, decision or understanding sought, narrative arc, evidence burden, and presenter-versus-self-serve use.

## Slide System Contract
Use a deliberate slide grammar with opening, section transitions, evidence, comparison, diagrams, tables/charts, recommendations, and close as appropriate. Avoid repeating one title-plus-card template across the deck.

## Content Density
Each slide must have one dominant communication job. Long prose belongs in notes or supporting documents unless the deck is explicitly designed to be read asynchronously.

## Evidence & Truth
Citations, metrics, claims, screenshots, logos, and customer proof must be supplied, generated as clearly labeled sample content, or sourced. Do not fabricate evidence.

## Output Integrity
Verify slide bounds, cropping, font fallback, contrast, chart legibility, image quality, speaker-note expectations, and exported PPTX/PDF rendering when those formats are produced.

## Slide Fit Preflight
<!-- id: contract.artifact.presentation-deck.slide-fit -->
Before freeze, render every slide and verify title/subtitle/body regions do not collide, clip, or escape their intended bounds. Long titles MUST be reflowed, resized within the approved type scale, or compositionally restructured rather than allowed to intrude into adjacent content. Direct slide-fit evidence MUST cover every slide, including title-box fit, subtitle separation, body/visual overlap, edge clipping, and rendered readability.
