# Email / Campaign Production Contract
<!-- id: contract.artifact.email-campaign -->

## Campaign Job
Resolve audience, trigger, stage, desired action, send sequence, timing assumptions, personalization inputs, and measurement intent.

## Message Architecture
Every email requires a clear job, subject/preheader relationship, scannable hierarchy, credible CTA, truthful proof, and fallback/plain-text considerations where relevant.

## Lifecycle & State Contract
For sequences, define entry, branching, suppression, stop conditions, conversion, re-entry, and failure/bounce considerations without inventing unavailable automation.

## Compliance & Truth
Do not invent consent, subscriber status, offers, scarcity, customer claims, or legal compliance. Route applicable marketing/privacy requirements.

## Evidence
Verify links, mobile rendering, dark-mode resilience, alt text, personalization fallbacks, unsubscribe/footer expectations, and sequence consistency.
